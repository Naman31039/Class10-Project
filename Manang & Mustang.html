Introduction

Manang and Mustang are among the most beautiful and adventurous areas of Nepal. They are known for mountains, dry landscapes, villages and trekking trails. These regions are very popular with trekkers and nature lovers.

Attractions and Highlights

  • Muktinath: A sacred place for both Hindus and Buddhists.
  • Jomsom: A beautiful valley near the Himalayan region.
  • Annapurna region: Famous for trekking and mountain views.
  • Local villages: Known for culture, mountain life and hospitality.

Tourism Activities

  1. Trekking in the Annapurna region
  2. Mountain photography and sunrise views
  3. Visiting sacred temples and villages
  4. Exploring local culture and lifestyle
  5. Adventure tourism and nature travel
