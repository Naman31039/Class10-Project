Introduction

Kathmandu is the capital city of Nepal and one of the most important cultural and historical destinations in South Asia. It is famous for heritage sites, temples, market areas and traditional architecture.

Important Tourist Places

  • Pashupatinath Temple: One of the most sacred Hindu temples in Nepal.
  • Kathmandu Durbar Square: A historic palace square with old architecture.
  • Swayambhunath: A famous Buddhist stupa also called the Monkey Temple.
  • Boudhanath: One of the largest stupas in the world.

Tourism Activities

  1. Temple visits and cultural sightseeing
  2. Photography of old buildings and monuments
  3. Shopping in local markets
  4. Trying local food and cultural music
