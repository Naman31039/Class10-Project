Introduction

Chitwan National Park is one of the most famous wildlife parks in Nepal. It is located in the southern part of the country and is famous for rhinos, tigers, birds and jungle scenery. Many tourists visit Chitwan to enjoy nature and adventure.

Wildlife and Attractions

  • One-horned rhinoceros: A famous animal of the park.
  • Bengal tiger: A major attraction for wildlife lovers.
  • Jungle safari: A popular activity in the forest.
  • Canoeing: A calm way to observe birds and animals.
  • Tharu culture: Local community life adds rich cultural value.

Tourism Activities

  1. Jungle safari and jeep rides
  2. Canoeing on the river
  3. Nature photography and bird watching
  4. Traditional Tharu dance and cultural programs
  5. Night jungle walks
