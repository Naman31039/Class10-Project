Welcome to Nepal

Nepal is a beautiful country in South Asia. It is famous for the mountains, temples, festivals, heritage sites and warm hospitality of its people. Every year, tourists visit Nepal for travel, culture, spirituality and adventure.

About Nepal

Nepal lies between China in the north and India in the south. The capital city of Nepal is Kathmandu. The country is famous for Mount Everest and many other high mountains. Nepal is also known for its valleys, rivers, lakes and green forests.

Nepal has a rich culture and many festivals. Dashain, Tihar, Holi and Losar are some of the most important festivals. The people of Nepal speak Nepali, and many local languages are also spoken in different regions. The natural beauty, peaceful monasteries, temples and adventure sports make Nepal a popular tourist destination.

Important Points

  • Home to the highest mountain ranges in the world
  • Beautiful culture, temples and traditions
  • Lots of natural beauty and scenic places
  • Good for trekking, boating and wildlife tourism
  • Popular for spiritual and religious travel

Major Tourist Activities

  1. Mountain trekking and hiking
  2. Boating on lakes and rivers
  3. Wildlife safari and jungle tours
  4. Religious visits and pilgrimages
  5. Paragliding, rafting and sightseeing

Tourism is important for Nepal because it creates jobs, supports local businesses and helps people know more about the country and its traditions. It also helps the country earn income from visitors who come to admire its beauty.
