Introduction

Pokhara is one of the most beautiful cities in Nepal. It is surrounded by hills and mountains and is famous for lakes, adventure activities and scenic views. Visitors often come here for relaxation and excitement at the same time.

Major Attractions

  • Phewa Lake: A famous lake where tourists enjoy boating and peaceful views.
  • Sarangkot: A hill station best known for sunrise and mountain views.
  • World Peace Pagoda: A great place for peace, meditation and photography.
  • Davis Falls: A waterfall with a unique underground flow.
  • Gupteshwor Cave: A natural cave near the falls.

Tourism Activities

  1. Boating on Phewa Lake
  2. Paragliding over the valley
  3. Sunrise viewing from Sarangkot
  4. Walking and photography in the hills
  5. Enjoying local food and culture
